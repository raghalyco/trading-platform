Previous Support Bounce

Ascending trendline / multi-touch horizontal support · Smart Money buy zone · loading…

Finds stocks bouncing off a previously-established support level (an ascending trendline or a horizontal level touched 2+ times before). Applies a full trader-style review before surfacing a setup: bearish structure (CHoCH/BOS) is excluded, RSI already overbought is excluded, and a breakout with weak volume + a weak close is treated as a likely fakeout and excluded. Shows Entry/SL (=invalidation)/T1/T2/R:R for surviving setups — SM score reflects structure, volume, and momentum context together, not just distance from support.

Episodic Pivot (Delayed EP)

Neglected stock + surprise-reaction day + volume shock, then a tight-range pullback candle · loading…

Ankur Patel's method: a stock that did nothing (no big move) suddenly reacts to a surprise — results, a new order, a policy change, promoter change — with a big one-day move (5.5%+) on unusually heavy volume ("day-0", >=300% of its own 50-day average). Because Indian circuit filters make the reaction candle itself unsafe to buy, this waits for a tight range-contraction pullback candle afterward: its high is the entry, its low the stop. WATCHING = pullback candle formed (or day-0 just happened), not broken out yet. TRIGGERED = it broke out recently, not gapped-and-chased. "Target" is a technical projection (day-0's own candle range, projected from entry) and gates what shows here (stop capped at 4%, R:R floor 1.5) — "1:3 (book 50%)" is Ankur's own stated convention (book half there, trail the rest below the 20 EMA) shown for reference, it doesn't gate the list.

Episodic Pivot — Backtest (6M) CACHED

Historical trade log using the exact live scanner rules, applied to every past day-0 → pullback → breakout sequence · loading…

Every trade here used the SAME entry/stop/target gates as the live "Episodic Pivot" tab (config.py's EP_* thresholds) — the only difference is this looks at every qualifying historical setup in the last 6 months, not just each stock's most recent one. Two exit variants are shown because daily bars can't tell us the true intraday sequence on the entry day itself: CONSERVATIVE checks the stop before the target starting the entry day (worst case); OPTIMISTIC skips the stop check on the entry day only (upper bound). Total Return / Max Drawdown assume a fixed 1% of capital risked per trade, summed without compounding — this run had up to - trades open at once, so a compounding curve would overstate returns. This is a signal-log backtest: no slippage, brokerage, or circuit-filter unfillability is modeled, and it should be read alongside the full written report, not as a standalone verdict.
